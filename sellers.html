The Selling Process

Move forward.
With confidence.

A strong sale is a series of well-made decisions. Each one builds on the last.

  1. Before anything goes to market, Geoff and Katrinna walk through your home with both a buyer's eye and an inspector's instinct. Identifying the right improvements early — and skipping the ones that won't pay off — can make a measurable difference in your final result.

  2. Pricing is part analysis, part intuition, part local knowledge. Too low leaves money on the table. Too high erodes buyer confidence and extends your days on market. The right price generates momentum — and momentum drives the kind of competition that produces the strongest offer.

  3. Professional photography and staging shape every buyer's first impression — and first impressions happen online, before a showing is ever booked. Thoughtful presentation frames your home's best qualities for the buyers most likely to compete for it.

  4. A well-timed launch builds anticipation before the listing goes live. The Smith Dobrowsky Team coordinates MLS exposure, agent network outreach, digital placement, and marketing materials so the right buyers discover your home from day one — not day fifteen.

  5. An offer is the beginning of the conversation, not the end. Geoff and Katrinna bring calm, experienced judgment to every negotiation — protecting your position and your result without letting a winnable deal fall apart over things that can be resolved.

  6. From accepted offer to keys out, there are dozens of details to manage — conditions, inspections, timelines, and paperwork. The Smith Dobrowsky Team stays present at every stage so nothing is left to chance before you reach the closing table.
