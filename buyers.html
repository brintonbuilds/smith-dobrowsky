The Buying Process

Move with clarity.
Not urgency.

Buying is a series of decisions. The ones made early shape everything that follows.

  1. Before you search, you need a number you can trust — not a range, not an estimate. The Smith Dobrowsky Team works through your budget and financing so that every home you look at is actually within reach.

  2. Not every neighbourhood ages the same way. Before the search begins, Geoff and Katrinna give you the local perspective that changes what a listing is worth — which streets hold value, which areas are shifting, and why two similar homes can have very different futures.

  3. When you find a home you're drawn to, Geoff and Katrinna help you understand what it's actually worth — not just what it's listed for. That evaluation shapes whether to offer, how to structure it, and what to ask before committing.

  4. A strong offer is rarely just about price. The Smith Dobrowsky Team builds the offer around your position, the seller's situation, and the conditions that protect you — so you're competitive without being reckless.

  5. Conditions and inspections exist for a reason. Geoff and Katrinna help you understand which protections matter most in the current market and how to structure your due diligence so nothing is left to chance before conditions come off.

  6. By the time you're closing, every detail should already be handled. The Smith Dobrowsky Team stays present through every stage so you're not discovering surprises at the table.
