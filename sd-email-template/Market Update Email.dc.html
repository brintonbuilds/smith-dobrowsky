How it stacks
Headshot moves above the greeting, full-width text below it.
Body copy rises to 17px / 1.75 line height on screens under 620px.
Snapshot stays a 2 × 2 grid — two stats per row, never four narrow columns.
Market KPIs hold three columns; values are short enough to fit 109px.
Each infographic is followed by a written summary and a link, so the section still works when the graphic is too small to read or images are switched off.
Buttons become full-width tap targets, 48px tall — same size and pill styling everywhere.
Footer columns stack; logos sit below the signature.
Uppercase labels are 11–12px minimum at #6f767e on cream (4.6:1).
