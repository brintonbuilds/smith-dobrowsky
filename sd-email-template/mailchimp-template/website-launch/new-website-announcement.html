Markets are usually described in averages. But the difference between two streets can matter more than the difference between two cities.

So each month, I’ll send you the part the averages hide: what’s really shifting in Burlington and Hamilton, where buyers and sellers each have the advantage, and what I’d do about it. Plain English, and never more than a few minutes to read.
