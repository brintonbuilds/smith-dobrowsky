01 Buying my first home

Where should a first home start?

You don’t need every answer before you begin. Most first searches come down to two questions: what can I comfortably afford, and where would I actually like to live? Start there.

Tell me which areas would work for you and what you need at home. We can compare a condo and a townhouse, including what the monthly fees cover, before you spend a Saturday at showings.
