What happened in August 2026

Overall sales declined 18.9% year over year, the median price fell 4.9% to $679,950 and the average declined 5.0% to $738,025. Homes took 43 days to sell, up 16.2%. Overall inventory was down 11.8%, the counterbalance keeping Hamilton balanced despite softer demand.

What it means for you

If you’re buying

August gave buyers a little more leverage — especially in condos.

Sales fell 18.9% year over year and homes took 43 days to sell, up 16.2%, giving buyers more time and negotiating room. The townhouse and condo median dropped 14.5% to $543,000. But inventory is still contracting — if activity recovers this fall, that advantage could disappear quickly.

If you’re selling

Lower inventory helps, but soft demand means expectations need to be realistic.

Inventory is down in both segments — detached 11.1% and condo 12.9% — which is helping support prices. Detached homes remain more resilient, with the median down just 2.6% to $740,000. Homes are taking longer to sell, so pricing and presentation are critical.

Common questions

Is Hamilton a buyer’s or seller’s market in August 2026?

Hamilton scored 49 out of 100 on the Smith Dobrowsky Market Balance Index™ for August 2026: balanced market, slightly buyer favoured. Weaker demand, lower prices and longer selling times outweighed tightening inventory, tipping the index marginally to the buyer’s side of balanced.

What was the median price of a detached home in Hamilton in August 2026?

The median detached sale price in Hamilton was $740,000, down 2.6% from a year earlier. The townhouse and condo median was $543,000 (down 14.5%).

How long are homes taking to sell in Hamilton?

In August 2026, detached homes in Hamilton averaged 40 days on market (up 11.1% year over year), and townhouses and condos averaged 51 days (up 27.5%).
