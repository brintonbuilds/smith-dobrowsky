What happened in October 2026

October detached sales eased 10.1% versus October 2025 and inventory fell 18.3%, while the detached median rose 4.0% to $1,206,000 and homes sold in 29 days. Townhouse and condo sales were flat, with a median of $700,000 (down 0.7%) and 36 days on market. Months of supply are 2.9 for detached and 3.9 for townhouse and condo.

Common questions

Is Burlington a buyer’s or seller’s market in October 2026?

Burlington scored 53 out of 100 on the Smith Dobrowsky Market Balance Index™ for October 2026: balanced market, mixed conditions, slightly favouring sellers. Detached sales eased 10.1% while inventory fell 18.3% — a balanced market with firmer detached prices.

What was the median price of a detached home in Burlington in October 2026?

The median detached sale price in Burlington was $1,206,000, up 4.0% from a year earlier. The townhouse and condo median was $700,000 (down 0.7%).

How long are homes taking to sell in Burlington?

In October 2026, detached homes in Burlington averaged 29 days on market (down 25.6% year over year), and townhouses and condos averaged 36 days (down 20.0%).
