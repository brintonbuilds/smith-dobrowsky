What happened in October 2026

October detached sales fell 5.6% to 304 and the median eased 5.0% to $702,500, with homes selling in 37 days. Townhouse and condo sales fell 12.4% to 120, with a median of $535,000 (down 13.4%) and 48 days on market. Inventory fell in both segments, with 4.4 months of supply for detached and 5.4 for townhouse and condo, helping keep Hamilton balanced.

What it means for you

If you’re buying

Lower prices and more time to negotiate, but fewer homes to choose from.

The detached median fell 5.0% to $702,500 and the townhouse and condo median fell 13.4% to $535,000, with sales lower in both segments. Inventory also fell (13.2% detached, 17.2% townhouse and condo), so buyers should weigh lower prices against reduced choice.

Common questions

Is Hamilton a buyer’s or seller’s market in October 2026?

Hamilton scored 45 out of 100 on the Smith Dobrowsky Market Balance Index™ for October 2026: balanced market, slightly buyer favoured. Lower sales and prices outweighed tighter inventory, leaving a balanced market that slightly favours buyers.

What was the median price of a detached home in Hamilton in October 2026?

The median detached sale price in Hamilton was $702,500, down 5.0% from a year earlier. The townhouse and condo median was $535,000 (down 13.4%).

How long are homes taking to sell in Hamilton?

In October 2026, detached homes in Hamilton averaged 37 days on market (down 5.7% year over year), and townhouses and condos averaged 48 days (down 4.3%).
