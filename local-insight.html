01 · Where do you fit?

Townhouses and condos offer more room to negotiate, while detached homes remain well supported.

Overall sales slowed 14.1% year over year, and the townhouse and condo median softened 8.2% to $670,000 with homes taking 13.5% longer to sell (42 days). Detached supply is tighter at 3.1 months of inventory and detached homes sold 23.5% faster, so well-priced detached listings still attract serious interest.

  • Best opportunityTownhouse / condo segment
  • WatchDetached inventory down 19.6%
  • Geoff’s adviceKnow where you have room to negotiate, and where you don’t

Detached sellers have the stronger hand, while condo and townhouse sellers face a softer market.

Detached sales fell 19.8%, but inventory dropped 19.6% and the median detached price rose 1.8% to $1,190,000, with homes selling in 26 days. Townhouse and condo sellers face softer prices and longer marketing times. With buyers selective, realistic pricing and strong presentation matter most.

  • Strongest advantageTight detached supply and faster sales
  • WatchSofter townhouse and condo prices
  • Geoff’s adviceRealistic pricing, strong presentation, neighbourhood-level knowledge
02 · Dig Deeper View the full September 2026 data

The Smith Dobrowsky Market Balance Index™ reflects market conditions based on supply, demand, pricing, competition, and momentum. Burlington’s September 2026 score is 55 out of 100.

Overall sales fell 14.1% year over year, led by a 19.8% drop in detached sales, while active listings fell 15.8%. Overall months of supply sit at 3.6, with detached at 3.1 months and townhouse/condo at 4.1 — two distinct market dynamics. A recovery in demand combined with limited inventory could strengthen competition, particularly for detached homes.

01 · Where do you fit?

Buyers have more time and more room to negotiate, with improved affordability.

Overall sales fell 22.6% year over year and the median price declined 8.9% to $640,000. Detached homes averaged 44 days on market (up 18.9%) and townhouses and condos 51 days (up 10.9%), giving buyers time to compare. Inventory is down 14.5%, and conditions can differ significantly between neighbourhoods, property types and price ranges.

  • Best opportunityLonger selling times in both segments
  • WatchInventory down 14.5% overall
  • Geoff’s adviceTake time to compare, and negotiate on price and condition

Reduced inventory helps, but buyers are comparing carefully, so expectations need to be realistic.

Inventory is down in both segments — detached 12.6% and condo 17.8% — which is preventing a bigger imbalance. But buyers are less inclined to overlook condition or pricing concerns, and homes are taking longer to sell. Well-presented homes priced for their neighbourhood still draw serious interest.

  • Strongest advantageFewer competing listings
  • WatchLonger days on market in both segments
  • Geoff’s advicePrice for your neighbourhood and present the home well

Active inventory was down 14.5%, with 4.8 months of inventory overall — broadly balanced, but noticeably softer than a year earlier.
