Common questions

Is Hamilton a buyer’s or seller’s market right now?

Hamilton scored 47 out of 100 on the Smith Dobrowsky Market Balance Index™ for September 2026: balanced market, leaning toward buyers. Lower sales, softer prices and longer selling times leaned the market toward buyers, while tighter inventory kept it from tipping out of balance.

What was the median price of a detached home in Hamilton in September 2026?

The median detached sale price in Hamilton was $692,500, down 9.9% from a year earlier. The townhouse and condo median was $565,000 (down 5.8%).
