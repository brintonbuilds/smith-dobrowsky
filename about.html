How We Work

Six things you can count on.

  1. The first conversation is about what you are trying to do and by when — not about a property.

  2. The most expensive decisions tend to get made early and quickly. We would rather take an extra week at the beginning.

  3. If the timing is wrong, or the price is not there, you will hear it from us before you hear it from the market.

  4. Two similar homes can have very different futures. That difference is local, and it is learnable.

  5. An offer is the beginning of the conversation, not the end. We protect your position without letting a workable deal fall apart.

  6. One team, one point of contact, from the first call through to closing. No handoffs to someone you have never met.
