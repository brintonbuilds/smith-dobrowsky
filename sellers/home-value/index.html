Questions

Before you reach out.

Rather talk it through? Call or text Geoff at 905.512.0301.

Do I need to be ready to sell?

No. Many people ask simply because they’re curious, or because they’re planning a year or more ahead. Knowing where your home may fit can help you decide whether and when a move makes sense.

Is there a cost for the initial review?

No. The initial review is free and comes with no obligation.

How accurate is a preliminary home value range?

A preliminary range is Geoff’s opinion based on the information available and recent local market activity. It is not a formal appraisal and not a guaranteed sale price. Seeing the home, and knowing about its condition and any updates, usually makes the range more meaningful.

Will Geoff need to visit my home?

Not to start. Geoff may ask a few questions first. If a visit would give you a clearer picture, he’ll suggest it, and it’s entirely your choice.

Am I committing to listing my property?

No. Asking for a review doesn’t commit you to anything, and you’re free to decide what, if anything, happens next.

Can I request a review if I live outside Burlington?

Yes. Geoff works across Burlington, Hamilton and surrounding communities, including Waterdown and Oakville. If your home is outside the area he knows well, he’ll tell you so honestly.
